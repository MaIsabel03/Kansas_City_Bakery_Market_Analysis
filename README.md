# **Kansas City Bakery Market Analysis** 

## **Project Overview** 

This project analyzes the Kansas City bakery market to identify customer preferences, competitive patterns, geographic trends, and potential opportunities for a Colombian-inspired bakery.

The project combines data collection, data cleaning, Python analysis, Excel, and Tableau visualization to turn multiple data sources into business insights and recommendations.

The analysis was completed as part of a data visualization project and focuses on using data to support a potential bakery business decision.

## **Business Question** 

How can available market and customer data be used to identify opportunities for a Colombian-inspired bakery in the Kansas City market?

The analysis focuses on:

- Customer ratings and review activity
- Bakery products and offerings
- Competitive positioning
- Geographic distribution of bakeries
- Business and food-service data
- Potential market opportunities

## **Data Sources**

The project uses multiple sources of data, including:

- Kansas City, Missouri food permit data
- Kansas City, Missouri business license data
- Bakery ratings and review counts
- Bakery addresses and location information
- Bakery product and offering information collected during the research process

The KCMO datasets were used to provide business and food-service information, while bakery-level data was collected and analyzed to evaluate the competitive landscape.

## **Data Preparation**

Multiple datasets were processed and combined as part of the analysis.

Python was used to:

- Load and process the KCMO datasets
- Clean and prepare the data
- Combine relevant datasets
- Organize bakery information for analysis
- Support the creation of the final cleaned dataset

The cleaned bakery dataset is provided in:

`Cleaned_data_Bakeries.xlsx`

The original and processed datasets are included in the `Datasets and Python codes` folder.

## **Analysis** 

The analysis examined bakery characteristics, customer ratings, review activity, product offerings, and geographic patterns across the Kansas City market.

Key areas of analysis included:

- Bakery ratings
- Number of customer reviews
- Product and menu offerings
- Specialty and ethnic bakery presence
- Geographic concentration of bakeries
- Competitive positioning
- Potential opportunities for Colombian-inspired products

## **Key Findings** 

The analysis identified several patterns in the Kansas City bakery market.

### **Customer Preferences** 

Cakes, almond croissants, and cinnamon nut rolls were among the products frequently mentioned in the bakery data and received strong customer ratings.

### **Specialty & Ethnic Bakeries** 

Specialty and ethnic bakeries demonstrated strong customer ratings, suggesting potential demand for differentiated bakery offerings.

### **Colombian Products**

Colombian bakery products such as pandebonos, pastelitos, and empanadas were less represented across the analyzed bakery offerings.

This indicated a potential opportunity to introduce differentiated Colombian-inspired products into the market.

### **Geographic Opportunities**

The analysis identified greater bakery concentration in areas such as Downtown and Midtown Kansas City, while other areas showed lower levels of bakery presence.

This geographic variation was considered when evaluating potential opportunities and market positioning.

## **Business Recommendations**

Based on the analysis, the project identified opportunities for a Colombian-inspired bakery to differentiate itself through:

- Specialty Colombian bakery products
- A combination of traditional Colombian offerings and familiar bakery products
- Strategic product positioning
- Consideration of areas with lower bakery concentration
- Differentiation through unique products and cultural offerings

The findings suggest that combining differentiated products with an appropriate location and market positioning could provide an opportunity within the Kansas City bakery market.
